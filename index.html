The Problem · المشكلة

Subscriptions quietly drain your budget.

We surveyed 221 young adults and professionals in Saudi Arabia. The results revealed a clear, painful pattern — users lose control of their subscriptions long before they notice.

bolt
0%

Unexpected charges

Forgotten subscriptions silently renew and surprise users with bills they didn't anticipate.

0%

No renewal reminders

Almost every user wants timely renewal alerts — but no existing tool provides them out of the box.

visibility_off
0%

Unaware of top costs

Nearly half of users can't tell which single subscription costs them the most each month.

view_module
AR/EN

Fragmented & not Arabic

Existing tools juggle multiple platforms with no unified view — and almost none support Arabic RTL.

The Solution · الحل

One hub for every subscription.

Wasla centralises your digital and physical memberships, sends smart reminders before charges hit, and answers your questions through an Arabic-first AI assistant.

Roadmap · خارطة الطريق

From prototype to a smarter future.

Phase 1 · GP1 — Completed

Core Subscription Management

CRUD for subscriptions, dashboard with spending, basic reminders, login flow. Validated in think-aloud tests with 5 users.

Phase 2 · GP2 — Completed

AI Chatbot · Smart Offers · Cancellation Guide

Deterministic Arabic chatbot, smart offers (Netflix 15%, Fitness 10%), step-by-step cancellation flow, reminder editing, improved UI.

Phase 3 — Future

Bank Integration · Auto-Import

Connect bank and payment platforms to auto-discover subscriptions and detect silent renewals.

Phase 4 — Future

Advanced AI · Marketplace · Social

Spending predictions, subscription marketplace, family sharing, smarter recommendations.
